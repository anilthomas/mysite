A little about me
"Technology is not just my profession — it is my passion, my classroom, and my way of giving back."

Over nearly two decades in the field, I have come to believe that the greatest privilege in technology is not just solving complex problems — it is sharing what you have learned so others can stand on your shoulders.

I approach every new technology — whether it is a new cloud platform, a container orchestration tool, or a storage architecture — with the same curiosity I had on day one. That hunger to understand things deeply, not just make them work, is what has kept me growing through every shift the industry has taken.

I mentor engineers not by giving answers, but by teaching them how to find them — how to read logs methodically, think in systems, and build the confidence to tackle the unknown. Because the best lesson I ever learned in tech is this: the problem in front of you is never as scary as the one you haven't seen yet.
